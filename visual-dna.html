Blue · white · coral

Roughly 64 / 34 / 2. Blue holds the environment, white holds the content, coral marks the heart. Coral never fills a surface, never tints a section, never appears twice in one glance.

Text on blue is white at full strength. White at reduced opacity is allowed for one supporting line only, never for a paragraph the reader has to work through.

03

Card anatomy

The card is the only container this brand has. Everything else on the website is a card that has been given a job.

הגשה לשולחן

או

בופה

Liver Production

proportion

5 : 7 (0.714). Portrait always. Landscape cards do not exist in this brand.

cardRadius

6% of the short edge — 18px on a 300px card. Inner rule radius 11px, so the two curves stay concentric. Never a pill, never a square corner.

cardBorder

1.5px solid brandBlue, inset 14px from the edge (4.7% of width). This inset rule — not the paper edge — is the brand's frame. On white grounds the paper edge adds a 1px rgba(79,107,165,.16) hairline so the sheet still reads.

heart

Sits centred on the top rule, interrupting it. Height ≈ 5% of card height. The rule breaks; the heart does not overlap type.

cardShadow

0 10px 26px rgba(31,44,74,.18). One shadow, blue-tinted, soft, directional down. Never layered, never spread wide, never on a white-on-white panel at rest.

interior

Question at the top, illustration floating in the middle third, signature at the foot. Roughly 45% of the interior is empty. That emptiness is the brand.

Small ±2°, medium ±{{ rotationMedium }}°. Rotation is only ever applied to a group of two or more cards, alternating sign, and never to a card holding more than four lines of text. Nothing in this brand is rotated for decoration.

Composition principles

Two-part, unequal. The box back's own layout: object on one side, words on the other, weighted about 40/60. Copied directly into the website's section grammar.

Loose cluster. Cards overlap slightly and rotate in alternating directions, as if set down by hand. Never a tidy row of three identical cards on an even grid.

Single centred subject. Reserved for the heart and the wordmark — one moment per page, flanked by hairlines, surrounded by empty blue. Everything else is asymmetric.

05

Illustration language

placeholder
commission in
the same family

Rules

Single continuous outline, no fill, no shading, no second colour. Stroke 2px at a 120px drawing — scale the stroke with the drawing so the weight stays optically constant (1.5px at 90px, 2.5px at 160px). Round caps and round joins. One subject per drawing, drawn slightly imperfectly, floating with no ground line or frame. Blue only; the heart is the single exception in coral.

Not this

No icon library standing in for a prominent illustration. No filled glyphs, no duotone, no two-weight strokes, no geometric grid-built icons, no isometric or 3D. Small functional UI marks (arrow, close, menu) may be simple 2px strokes from the same pen, but they are furniture, not illustration — the three drawings above show what the reader should recognise as the brand's hand.

06

The fingerprint heart

One motif, three permitted uses

1 · Full asset. Large, coral, on blue, with the love script ring intact. One per page maximum, and only where the page is otherwise empty.

2 · Card seal. Small solid coral heart centred on a card's top rule, breaking it. Marks a thing as a card. No ring, no text.

3 · Favicon / app mark. The heart alone, no wordmark.

Never

Never as a bullet, a loading spinner, a hover cursor, a background pattern, a section divider or a repeated texture. Never recoloured, never outlined, never rotated, never cropped, never placed on a photograph. Never animated beyond a single slow fade or draw-in. It is a signature — it loses all of its value the moment it appears twice.

07

Interface, derived

Buttons

A button is a small card: 1.5px blue rule, 8px radius, 44px tall, wide letter-spacing, generous horizontal padding. No pill, no gradient, no shadow at rest.

Coral appears in exactly one button state: the underline of the quiet tertiary link. Primary is blue on blue-bordered paper, because that is how the cards are printed.

Interactive card

Hover the cards. Rest: −2°, soft shadow. Hover: rotation relaxes to 0°, lifts 6px, shadow deepens. The gesture is picking a card up off a table, not a UI element glowing.

08

Motion

motionFast

140ms — press, focus, rule draw

motionNormal

260ms — card lift, hover, colour

motionSlow

520ms — card flip, section entrance, heart fade

motionEasing

cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) — quick out, settles like paper

Every movement in this brand is something happening to a card: lifting, turning over, being dealt, settling. Motion is allowed only when it carries product meaning. No parallax, no scroll-triggered decoration, no counters, no looping ambient drift, no bounce.

Brand fidelity test

Each element on this board was checked by printing it, mentally, onto a physical card beside the supplied product. The blue and coral are the sampled values unchanged. The rule weight, radius ratio, inset and shadow are measured from the artwork rather than chosen. The spacing is deliberately looser than a website would normally be, because the cards are. The one thing still unresolved is typography: Heebo, Assistant and Caveat are stand-ins with the right personality, not matches — the Hebrew display face in particular needs the real name before the system can pass.
